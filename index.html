About the scores

Scores are model confidence, not resource quality. With no category selected, the highest “yes” score is used. Otherwise, confidence is the arithmetic mean of the selected categories’ “yes” scores. Results are always highest confidence first, with unscored records last. Records with all categories classified as “unknown” count as analysis errors.
